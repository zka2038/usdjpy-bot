Definitions
Gotobi days
The 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th, 25th and last day of each month, moved back to the previous Japanese business day when they fall on a holiday. Japanese importers settle on these days and buy dollars at the 09:55 JST fix.
Control days
All other Japanese business days. The same trade on these days shows how much of the result is just time-of-day drift.
Net pips
Profit per trade in pips (0.01 yen) after buying at the ask, selling at the bid, IBKR commission (0.2 bp, $2 minimum) and 0.1 pip slippage per side, on a $100k trade.
Pessimistic costs
Slippage raised to 0.3 pip per side, which takes 0.4 pip more off every round trip.
t-stat
Mean divided by its standard error. Around 2 or more means the mean is unlikely to be zero by chance.
Gotobi vs control
A Welch t-test on mid-price moves (no costs) between gotobi and control days. The p-value is the chance of a gap this large if the two were the same.
